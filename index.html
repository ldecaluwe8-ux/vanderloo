It started in the garden

You probably know the moment. Dinner is done, the dishes can wait, and you step outside for a minute. The grass is still damp from this afternoon's rain. Somewhere a window glows. The sky turns soft grey, and just above the roof, the first star appears.

That minute is small, but it's yours. Nobody needs anything from you. You breathe in, look up, and feel at home, in the garden and in yourself.

VANDERLOO was made to keep that feeling close. Not a scent for big occasions, but one you reach for every day: before work on a Monday, at home on a slow Sunday, on the first cold evening of autumn. One bottle, one scent, the whole year round.
